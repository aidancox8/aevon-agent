# Tempo: the zero-cost channel list

Paid conferences are off the table until Tempo has made money. That is the right call and the
research supports it: ClinicSense ran conferences **after** they had, in the founder's words,
"gathered enough clout and customers in the space". Buying a booth with no reference and no case
study is paying for a step that has not been earned.

Everything below costs nothing but time. All of it needs the Jane quotes in
[tempo-jane-gap.md](tempo-jane-gap.md), which is the thing that makes a stranger credible.

Ranked by leverage.

---

## 1. Manju Asdhir, Director of Partnerships, Clinic Accelerator

The single highest-leverage contact found. Rick Lau built and exited 127 PT/chiro clinics and
was COO of pt Health; Clinic Accelerator claims 1,000+ clinic owners in-program and a 30,000+
owner newsletter. Vendor access is already proven and priced: the Clinic Boss Show runs sponsor
reads with discount codes for Ginger Desk, Articulate Design and CallHero.

The approach is a partnership conversation, not a sponsorship purchase. Ask nothing that costs
money on the first email.

Draft, deliberately short:

> **Subject:** front desk shifts in Jane
>
> Manju,
>
> Jane's own docs say it can't schedule front desk staff and recommend clinics use an external
> calendar for it, and that it will let you double-book a treatment room. I built the thing that
> fills that gap. It runs in production at a 60-provider multidisciplinary clinic in BC.
>
> I'm not pitching a sponsorship. I want to know whether this is a problem your owners actually
> raise, or whether I'm solving something they've quietly lived with.
>
> Worth a short call?
>
> Aidan

## 2. PABC Clinic Management Support Network

PABC is building an operations support network for BC clinic managers right now. Being in the
room while they define their own tooling is worth more than any booth, and the task force is
forming, so the timing will not repeat. `info@bcphysio.org`

## 3. The Health of Business podcast (Danielle Boyd)

BC-specific, explicitly about clinic ownership in BC, open guest application, free. The Jane gap
is a genuinely good episode topic and it positions Aidan as the person who found it rather than
someone selling. `hello@danielleboyd.ca`

## 4. CPA Private Practice Division "Tech Tuesdays"

2,000+ clinic owners nationally, a recurring content slot that needs content.
`privatepracticedivision@gmail.com`

## 5. Jane Integrations intake form

The programme is explicitly "at capacity" and there is no open API, and the developer platform
has no endpoints for shifts, rooms or availability, so this cannot be a 2026 channel. Fill the
form anyway to sit in the queue, then forget it.

## Cheap, if the free ones show signal

Do not spend here until one of the above produces a real conversation.

- **RMTBC e-newsletter, $100/week**, 3,000+ BC RMTs. Cheapest paid reach in BC by a distance.
- **CAOT Sponsored Educational Presentation, $995**, a one-hour webinar to 11,000+ members.
  Best value found anywhere, but it is still $995 and it is still pre-revenue.

## Explicitly not doing

Paid conference booths (AACM $1,200, BCCA, CAOT $2,100). Revisit only once there is a paying
clinic or a named reference. A booth converts attention into a conversation; with nothing to
point at, there is nothing for the attention to land on.
