(multidisciplinary, rehabilitation, pain management, physiotherapy). Solos are excluded
deliberately: a solo has no schedule problem.

These are NOT a send list. Nobody in the first-customers research got customer #1 from a
list larger than 100, and none of them got it by emailing. This is a call-and-visit list,
and the goal of each contact is a conversation, not a demo.


**Data warning:** roughly half these contact names are scraper artifacts, not people. "About
Langley" and "Certified Hand" are page headings, not humans. Budget 30 seconds per row on the
clinic website to get the real practice manager before calling.

Ask at the end of every conversation: "Who else do you know I should talk to?"
