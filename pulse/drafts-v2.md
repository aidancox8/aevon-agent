# Eight drafts, v2: rewritten against cold email research

2026-08-21. Supersedes `drafts-named.md`. Every draft there failed the same three checks.

## What the research says, and where v1 failed

| Finding | Source | v1 result |
|---|---|---|
| Subject under 4 words gets **8.9% reply, 4.2x** subject lines over 13 | Belkins, Gong, Lavender all agree on 1-4 words | 3 of 8 were over 4 words |
| Ideal body **25-50 words**; 50-125 still gets 8.2% | Lavender, Overloop | All 8 were 78-91 words. Acceptable band, not ideal |
| Short direct greeting, **"Hi [Name],"** or "[Name]," | Instantly greeting benchmarks | **0 of 8 had any greeting.** Straight into "Nathan," |
| **Timeline hooks 10.01% vs problem-statement hooks 4.39%**, a 2.3x gap | Instantly | v1 referenced their posting but framed it as a problem statement |
| Questions get **2x** replies over statements; low-commitment ask beats a meeting request | Gong | Passed. All 8 close on an easy-to-decline question |
| Asking for "thoughts" raises replies but **cuts meetings 20%** | Gong | Passed. None ask for thoughts |
| Personalisation moves reply **3% to 7%**; company-specific research **17-18%** | Mailmeteor, Instantly | Passed, and this is the campaign's whole advantage |
| Signature: name, company, one link. No logos, banners or icons | GMass, Salesforge | **0 of 8 had a signature at all** |
| Put social proof in the **signature**, not the body, to protect length | Salesforge | v1 spent ~12 body words per email on the clinic line |

**Net:** the arguments were right, the packaging was wrong. Fixes applied to all eight: a real greeting, subject cut to 2-3 words, body trimmed to 50-70 words, and the clinic reference moved out of the body into the signature so it stops eating the word budget.

## The signature, identical on all eight

Plain text only. No HTML, no anchor text, no logo. That combination is exactly what got the domain quarantined as phishing on 18 August, and a bare URL in plain text cannot mismatch its destination.

```
Aidan Cox
Aevon, Vancouver BC
Staff records, training and credentials in one system. Runs daily at a 75 staff clinic in BC.
aevon.ca
```

The sender appends the opt-out line automatically, so it is not repeated here.
