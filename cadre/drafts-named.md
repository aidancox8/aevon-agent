were addressed to nobody.

Ordered by how ready they are to send. Nothing here has been sent.

## What changed

Addressing a named person changes the copy, not just the greeting. "Your posting asks for
someone to track certifications" is a fact about a company. Said to the Corporate Health and
Safety Manager, it is a fact about **his** week. The second one gets a reply or a no, which are
both useful. The first gets ignored.

Two of these are addressed to a person at a generic inbox. That is fine and normal: the name in
the greeting does the work, and whoever screens `info@` forwards it.
